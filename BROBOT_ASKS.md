# Brobot Asks

The basic asks Brobot 1 and Brobot 2 know how to answer - type them in the chat, or hold a numpad key and say them. This page is made from the game's own topics (`goverlord/wire_pod_overlay/chipper/gopod_probes/chapters/`), so it changes when they do.

## Three ways to ask

| Ask | Example | You get |
|---|---|---|
| A topic on its own, or "tell me about" a topic | "Mobile gear?" · "Tell me about the rig" | The summary, riffed on - it shows in the Brobot's notice bubble. |
| "tell me all about", "all about", "tell me more" | "Tell me all about the rig" · "Tell me more" | The details, one value point at a time. "Tell me more" stays on the last topic. |
| A pointed question | "What's in the upper crate?" | The one value point it asks about. |

Every answer scores in the game: topics, value points and easter eggs, shown in the notice bubble.

**The probers** are Ron's own questions from real sessions - examples of how he thought, kept in the probers list (`goverlord/wire_pod_overlay/chipper/gopod_probes/probers_list/`, checked by `system-test-probers-list`).
