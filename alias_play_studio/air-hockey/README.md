# Air hockey and the stage
- **words:** air hockey, gohockey, gosnap, gostage, virtual stage, stage snapshot, gohockey-cal, gohockey-part, gohockey-rec, overhead camera, runcam, the stage
- **ask:** What are the air hockey and stage aliases?
- **about:** the terminal's air hockey and stage aliases (~/.gopod_alias_lib/brobots.sh); the overhead RunCam watches the table, the ruler bar is the arena's scale
---

## Tour
1. The camera moved? Say “gohockey-cal” - it finds the ruler and the track again, then stops. No robot is touched.
2. Then “gosnap” - one overhead picture of the stage, to see the Brobots, the cube and the chargers where they are.
3. Then “gohockey-part 1” - Brobot 1 wakes, leaves his charger and parks in front of his net, then stops. Add 2 for Brobot 2.
4. All good? “gohockey” plays the whole game in a window; “gohockey-rec” plays it and saves the video instead.

## Commandments
1. gosnap, one overhead picture of the stage, saved to gopod_notes/stage/
2. gohockey-cal, find the ruler and the track again (the camera moved), then stop - no robot touched
3. gohockey-part, one part of the game alone: 1|2|3 [2] (1: wake, leave home, park; 2 at the end: Brobot 2 plays) - wakes and drives the robots
4. gohockey, the whole game in a window: [2] (Brobot 2 plays) - wakes and drives the robots
5. gohockey-rec, the whole game saved to a video in gopod_notes/stage/: [2] - wakes and drives the robots
6. gostage-see, each Brobot by his marker (Brobot 1 red, Brobot 2 yellow lego) and his spot on stage, a marked picture saved to gopod_notes/stage/ - no robot touched
7. gostage, each Brobot off his charger and onto his spot: either side of the cube near the centre spot, or either side of the centre spot with room for the cube - wakes and drives the robots
