# Songs
- **words:** pha0b, score, score-save, tempo-set, phcal, gobingo, play a song, song score, tune a move, calibrate, tempo, chocolate bingo
- **ask:** What are the song aliases?
- **about:** the terminal's song aliases (~/.gopod_alias_lib/brobots.sh); in the chat, system-play, system-score, system-set <song> tempo and system-calibrate do the same jobs
---

## Tour
1. See a song first: “score” prints its score on the screen; “score-save” keeps the page in the song's notation folder.
2. Then “pha0b” - pick a song and play all of it, or just a range of steps, live or dry.
3. A move looks off? “phcal” - tune one move on one robot, one step at a time.
4. Too fast or too slow? “tempo-set” - the song's tempo.
5. The live game: “gobingo” - Chocolate Bingo, with Brobot 2's angry reactor alongside.

## Commandments
1. score, a song's score on the screen: [song] [narrative]
2. score-save, the score page saved in the song's notation/ folder: [song] [narrative]
3. pha0b, play a song or a range of it, live or dry: alone opens the song menu; or <song> <from step> <to step> [robot]
4. phcal, tune one move on one robot: alone walks you through it; or <move> <1|2> [values] - moves the robot
5. tempo-set, a song's tempo, walked through
6. gobingo, the live Chocolate Bingo game - Brobot 2's angry reactor runs alongside and stops with it
