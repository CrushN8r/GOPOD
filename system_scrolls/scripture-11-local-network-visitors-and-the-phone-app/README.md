## Tour
1. Phones and computers on your local network can join the chat - each shows up under "Local network visitors", with the count. Visitor chat participation starts OFF; turn it on to let visitors chat. Each visitor is listed with its name, like "Phone 2", then its network address, the device and the browser - and a Clear button to remove it. A visitor's first line is its username request: Approve or Clear it. An approved visitor has a box: unticked, it's muted.
2. The phone app is the same chat, on your phone, as a visitor on your network. Once: in Chrome, open GOPOD's address with /gopod_ca.crt and download it; in your phone's settings, search "CA certificate", Install anyway and pick it; restart Chrome. Then open GOPOD's secure address (port 8443) with ?app - no warning - and in Chrome's menu pick "Install app". Tap the GOPOD icon. On a new network, open the new address once and install again.
