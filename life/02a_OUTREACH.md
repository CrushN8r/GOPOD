# Outreach

How GOPOD goes out into the community, and how it earns its keep. Two separate
lanes, shown side by side, run on a handful of principles rather than an
endless checklist.

---

## The path so far

It started with one door: the **Windsor Downtown Mission (WDTM)** — and that
door was already open. GOPOD's creator came up through WDTM's **Enterprise**
program. So the return to WDTM isn't a cold pitch; it's a participant coming
back as a success story, bringing something built, to give back to the place
that helped it start.

That's what makes it a real community stage: a place where a warm brobot show
could do some good and prove the thing works in front of actual people — in a
room that already knows the story.

Before walking back through that door, the smart move was to get ready. That
meant eyeing **WEtech and the Small Business Centre (SBC)** — not as a stage,
but as a launch pad. They don't book the show; they sharpen the pitch, open
doors, and lend credibility. Preparation, not performance.

So the arc is simple: **start at WDTM, prepare through WEtech + SBC, return to
WDTM ready.** Everything else found along the way — more community partners,
and a separate paid lane — sorts cleanly into the map below.

The same borrowed-stage idea runs the other direction too, not just for the
founder — see [HEALTHY_DISTRACTIONS.md](02_HEALTHY_DISTRACTIONS.md) for how
someone with no platform of their own can borrow GOPOD's for a minute, the
same way GOPOD once borrowed WDTM's.

## Two lanes, side by side

These run in parallel. They can be shown together, pitched together, and live
on the same page. What they never do is lean on each other or bleed into each
other's story.

| | **Community lane** (trust) | **Paid lane** (revenue + buzz) |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Build trust, prove the good | Earn income, spread the buzz |
| **Where** | Missions, youth orgs, community hubs | Bars and comedy clubs (18+ rooms) |
| **Content** | The show, warm and clean | The same show, all-ages by design |
| **What sells it** | Doing genuine good, locally | It works commercially |

Two acts on one résumé — not one secret and one public.

## Community lane — the targets

- **WDTM** — the anchor. Where it started, still important. Approached
  prepared.
- **WEtech + SBC** — the launch pad. Sharpen the pitch, open the doors. The
  engine behind the rest.
- **STAG** — the easy warm stage. Youth, casual, simple to walk into. A good
  early live test.
- **New Beginnings** — the heavyweight reference. Long-established, work- and
  life-readiness overlap. Cited to funders; approached deliberately.
- **Mental Health Connections** — an adult, warm side-booking. Real good to be
  done there, but off the youth spine. On the list, not the center of it.
- **Windsor Police Service** — media/public affairs, public education. A real local
  public-safety org, a candidate door if road-safety content ever gets built. Not
  approached, nothing arranged.
- **Essex-Windsor EMS** — community relations. Same candidate status — a real door on
  the list, nothing arranged, nothing promised.

Clean sequence: **WEtech + SBC to sharpen → STAG to test warm → WDTM prepared →
New Beginnings and Mental Health Connections as ready.**

## Paid lane — the rooms

Bars and comedy clubs, 18+ rooms. Bingo travels here **all-ages by design** —
the room is adult, the material isn't. This lane is about revenue and buzz, not
trust-building, and it stays fully firewalled from the community story.

A separate, smaller idea sits alongside this lane, not inside it: a possible future
Cameo.com channel for personalized Doc/Pip comedy messages (see
[BIRTHDAY.md](../web/BIRTHDAY.md)) — a different revenue mechanism from a room booking,
not built, not launched.

## The print-shop offering (proposal, not yet pitched)

A concrete thing to bring back through the WDTM door, not just a show. GOPOD is already
drafting printable tools — Doc & Pip's math workbook, the answer keys, word-search
puzzles — see [EDUCATION.md](03_EDUCATION.md)'s "Print spinoff" note and
`life/DOCS_MATH_AFTERMATH.pdf` for the draft itself. The idea: produce them through
WDTM's own print shop instead of anywhere else.

- **WDTM** gets real print-shop business, not just a good story.
- **The tools** get an actual local production home instead of sitting as a PDF.
- **GOPOD** gets something concrete to hand WEtech, SBC, and Mental Health Connections
  beyond "come watch two robots talk" — a tangible, locally-made product with a
  values-aligned supply chain behind it.

Not pitched yet. A real offering worth having ready before the return to WDTM, not a
built partnership — the print shop's own capacity and willingness haven't been
confirmed, and the exact ask (one title, a small run, an ongoing line) isn't decided
here.

## How safeguards actually work

You can't pre-account for every case, and trying to is a trap. What holds up
isn't an exhaustive list — it's a few clean principles that generate the right
call in the moment:

- **All-ages content stays all-ages.** An adult venue is about the room, not
  the material.
- **Gambling is never raised.** If a venue or a patron brings it up, that's
  their door to open. GOPOD just has a fun brobot show.
- **The two lanes don't lean on each other.** A paid booking never becomes part
  of why a community partner says yes, and vice versa.

Those few rules cover the situations that actually come up far better than a
giant checklist would.

When something new turns up that the rules don't obviously answer, that's the
signal to stop and think about that **one** case — not to build the wall higher
in advance. **Handle edge cases when they actually knock.**

## Per-platform outreach lanes

The two lanes above are the strategy. This section is the execution, one platform at a
time — starting with Facebook, built so Reddit and other platforms slot in later as their
own sibling entries, not folded into a Facebook-only block. Same firewall as above applies
inside each platform too: no cross-posting between a platform's own trust and paid groups,
different door, different framing, every time.

**Golden takeaways:**
- **The firewall holds inside a platform too.** Trust-lane and paid-lane groups on the same
  platform never see the same post or the same framing — the per-platform split below is
  the two lanes above, applied one level deeper, not a new rule.
- **A side door beats a front door when the front door isn't open yet.** Food Spotters never
  gets a GOPOD pitch — it gets a real, native request (a custom cake) that happens to carry
  the brand in on its own. Foodmath stays the actual home; the group post is just the lure.
- **Reach isn't the only variable — framing control is.** Rant/Raves is left out specifically
  because 88K broad reach with no framing control is a worse trade than a smaller, sorted
  group. Bigger isn't automatically better.
- **Demo what's actually locked, not what's merely written.** Bingo leads every live demo
  (finished, 69/69) — Interview doesn't, because it can't fire standalone yet. A doc saying
  "confirmed live" is not the same claim as a human having watched it fire on the day.

### Facebook

**Trust lane — the groups** (feeds the Community lane above):
- Small Business Windsor Essex (YQG) — strongest fit
- Windsor's Young Professionals and Entrepreneurs
- Windsor Entrepreneur and Start-Up Collaboration
- Windsor's 3D Printing Group
- Windsor Essex-County Social & Networking
- People of Windsor/Essex and Surrounding Areas
- WEtech / Hackforge / GDG — pages and event calendars, not open groups; a different
  posting mechanism than the six groups above, same trust lane.

**Paid / adult-venue lane — the groups** (feeds the Paid lane above; licensed rooms —
bars, clubs, casinos, legit adults-only; direct owner/booker approach, "a live two-brobot
bit for your room"):
- Vendors, Events, and Booth Exhibitors of Windsor Essex County
- N&R Vendor Event Planners ~ Windsor & Essex County
- Per-post call in these two specifically: wearing the trust hat or the paid hat, since
  both crowds are genuinely present in both groups.

**Food side-door — IN, a different door entirely:**
- Food Spotters (both groups). Not a GOPOD demo post — post a photo of the Hot Wheels food
  cars, ask for a custom cake. The brand rides in on a real, native request; foodmath stays
  the actual home ([FOODMATH_AHA_MOMENT.md](../web/FOODMATH_AHA_MOMENT.md)), the group post
  is just the lure.
- The same trust-lane groups above (Small Business Windsor Essex especially) are also the
  path for approaching local businesses directly — foodmath's chocolate-bingo/cake side-door
  is one concrete way in, not the only one.

**OUT:**
- Rant/Raves — 88K broad reach, no framing control, easy wall-collapse. Left out.

Nothing else on Facebook is open right now.

**Demo-readiness truth, for whichever lane actually pitches or demos.** Lead any live
demo with **Bingo** (finished, 69/69 locked) — not Interview, which can't fire standalone
yet. Awaken is the locked second option. Don't trust a doc's own "confirmed/verified live"
wording as proof of readiness — only eyes-on-hardware, on the actual day, counts. Same
discipline as everywhere else in this project: say what runs, not what's merely written.
