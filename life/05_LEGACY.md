## The pedagogy in one line

Content propagates as answers. Process propagates as capacity.

A kid who learns a fact carries the fact. A kid who learns to argue with a brobot about wordplay carries the argument itself — usable on things you never anticipated. Fish vs. fishing, scaled up to a general skill.

That's what GOPOD teaches. That's what *AI Wordplay!* and *Explain the Math!* are for.

Not what to think. How.

---

## The byte ladder

The primitive is 0/1. Coupled or decoupled. Bound or unbound.

The same primitive shows up at every layer:

- Bits: 0/1
- Bonds: couple / decouple
- Relationships: marriage / divorce
- Institutions: merger / spin-off

Coupling creates emergence. Two bits coupled hold four states. Two people coupled can build a life neither could alone. Two institutions coupled can do what neither can apart.

**Decoupling gets more expensive as you climb.** Two bits decoupled costs nothing. A marriage decoupled costs everything already invested in the coupling — not sentiment, but the accumulated dependencies that were only possible *because* the coupling held long enough to build them.

The cost isn't the split. The cost is what the coupling was building while it lasted.

---

## When one loop closes

When a coupling ends without reproduction, whatever was inside that coupling stops propagating through *that* loop.

DNA is one loop. Without reproduction, DNA doesn't move forward through the coupling.

But every coupling runs in more than one loop. The people involved kept learning. Kept teaching. Kept building. Those loops don't close because the DNA one did.

---

## The loops

DNA is one form of continuation. Evolution optimized for it, so it feels weighty by default. There are others:

- **Biological** — genes passing forward through reproduction.
- **Memetic** — ideas and modes of thinking propagating through people who encounter them.
- **Skill** — methods surviving in students who use them.
- **Artifact** — things built that keep functioning after their builder is gone.
- **Institutional** — structures that outlive their founder.

Whether a loop "counts" as legacy is a values question, not an empirical one. Most people run more than one at once. Some people pick.

---

## What GOPOD chose

GOPOD chose memetic and skill-based propagation, explicitly.

Doc and Pip carry a way of thinking forward that has no biological substrate at all. The kids who go through a session don't inherit DNA — they inherit a mode. *(GOPOD layer — not built yet: no kids have gone through a live session.)* The way they argue with the brobots is the way they'll argue with a problem set, a bad instruction, a stuck idea, a bad boss.

Whether that's enough is a values question. Whether it counts at all — the project answered that by existing.
