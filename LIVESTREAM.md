# Livestream: AI Wordplay! Explain the MAth!

> Talking to an AI is already wordplay. You pick the words, you shape the ask, you get a result
> back - that's the whole move, every time. "AI Wordplay! Explain the MAth!" turns that move into
> a show: throw a confusing number or claim at the room, and watch people fight to explain it.

The livestream is where the Brobots and the room play it live. It hasn't run in front of a live
audience yet - this is the design.

## The loop

One segment. A claim or a number that doesn't sit right - a price that looks wrong, a stat that
can't be true, a rule nobody can explain. The room competes: funniest explanation, clearest
explanation, best analogy wins. The fun runs through the wrong answer on the way to the real one -
the funniest, most literal misreading gets floated and laughed at first, then the math underneath
settles it, so the joke and the lesson land in the same breath.

The winners get written up on Math Aftermath. That write-up and its clip become doorways - into a
pillar site, into the next contest round. Then the room hands over the next confusing thing, and
the loop runs again.

One session in. Many doorways out.

## The contest wheel

The topics aren't a list - they're a wheel. "This week: Survival Math." Next stretch, something
else. Nine everyday-math themes, each its own contest stretch:

- **Food** - kitchen, recipes, portions, cost per serving.
- **Money** - budgeting and everyday practical figures.
- **Sport** - scores, stats and comparisons.
- **Fashion** - cost per wear, sizing, wardrobe decisions.
- **Language** - meaning, rhythm, wordplay, timing.
- **Personality** - people, character, comedy, what fits a room.
- **Survival** - resources, risk and reward, working through a scenario.
- **Access** - maths made reachable for anyone who was told they were bad at it.
- **Aftermath** - the write-up after the fact: what happened and what it meant.

Nine themes, not nine businesses - one format, spun nine ways.
