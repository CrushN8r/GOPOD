# Channel

Lane 1 is the product for 2026–2028.

- **1a Niche buzz.** Show builders a polished studio and one song they can recognise as a format. Artifact: the listed Bingo test song.
- **1b Public machine.** YouTube library, Foodmath pages, then livestream. Money mix over 3–5 years: ads and memberships, Foodmath affiliates, table gigs.

1a and 1b share the car. They do not share the same week's labour.
