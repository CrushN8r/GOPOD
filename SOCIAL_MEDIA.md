## One platform, one job

- **YouTube** - video sparks, longer proof cuts, Shorts. The channel's goals are in
  [LIVESTREAM.md](LIVESTREAM.md).
- **TikTok** - fast clips, hooks, character moments.
- **Instagram** - reels, carousels, quote cards, CHALK's visual cards.
- **Pinterest** - durable visual search: the niche pillars, like foodmath - content aimed to be
  unique in a pool of AI slop.
- **Facebook, X, LinkedIn** - local updates, short sparks, professional proof.

Every post leads back to a real page, never the post itself: crushn8r.com routes people where they
need to go (the sites are listed in [LIVESTREAM.md](LIVESTREAM.md#where-it-lands)).

**One joke, many doorways.** One good moment from the livestream becomes a Short, a Reel, a TikTok, a
Math Aftermath write-up, a lesson - and the next contest prompt.
