# Scripture 5, Apply

Edits take effect after a restart, from Scripture 1. Edited story lines get their new voices made then.

“system-voices-rebuild” checks and backs up every voice, and the restart after it makes them all again. Then restart: go to Scripture 1.

## Commandments

- system-voices-rebuild, check, back up every voice, then restart makes them all again

*Then restart: Go to scripture 1.*
