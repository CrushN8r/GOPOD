# Scripture 8, Tune moves

A move looks off? Try it alone on one Brobot first. “system-calibrate” plays one move, an arm, a nod, the rattle or a cube light, with its speed, hold and cycles. Turn tweaks on in Scripture 6 to keep the tuned values.

“system-anim” tests an animation token with words. Then play that part in its song with system-play, from Scripture 2.

## Commandments

- system-calibrate, <arm|nod|rattle|danger|cube> <1|2> [reps] [--hold S] [--speed N] [--cycles N] [--volume N] [dry]
- system-anim list, the animation tokens
- system-anim, the animationWI tester (WIP): a token and words, e.g. angry with a count

*Play that part: system-play <song> from <step> to <step> (group 2).*
