# Scripture 6, Switches

Turn parts on or off for this session. “system-numpad” shows who each numpad key talks to; “on” makes the keys push-to-talk, “off” lets them type as usual. “system-gap” sets the reporter gap for system-play.

“system-tweaks on” plays the moves with their tuned values, “system-native” turns Wire-Pod's own voice answers on or off, and “system-record” turns Record Session on or off. When you're done, close the window with the speaker icon.

## Commandments

- system-numpad, each numpad key: who it talks to, live or not
- system-numpad on, numpad keys are push-to-talk
- system-numpad off, numpad keys type as usual
- system-gap, reporter gap for system-play: <seconds> | off | default
- system-tweaks on, apply phcal tweaks before a live system-play
- system-tweaks off, play the moves as written
- system-native on, Wire-Pod's own voice answers on
- system-native off, Wire-Pod's own voice answers off
- system-record on, Record Session on (the page's Record? box)
- system-record off, Record Session off, kept zipped
- system-robots-off on, Robots off: the Brobots' says on the page in their numpad voices, says and animations captured
- system-robots-off off, Robots back on

*Close the menu with the speaker icon.*
