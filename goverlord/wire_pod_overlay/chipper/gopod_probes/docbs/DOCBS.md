# DOCBS: the master cookbook of response recipes

Doc's DocBS answers, as recipes. Each recipe is one chain (`docbs.chains` in `webroot/gopod_settings/02_cast.json`): its beats in order, each beat a line from its pool here. The words are the operator's own: [the Master Library](DOCBS_LIBRARY.md) and the DocBS pools. Written 2026-10-30 from the settings and the pools as they were then; it doesn't update itself, so a changed chain or pool means this page gets written again.

## How every recipe is served

1. **The dial decides.** `docbs.share` (0-100, 110 = 11/10) is how often Brobot 1's answer is DocBS, and which recipes are on the menu: a recipe needs its `min_share`.
2. **One recipe per answer,** from a shuffled deck: never the same recipe twice until the deck runs out. Every pool line works the same way.
3. **The recipe's beats,** each its own thought bubble.
4. **His reply:** the value point, in his colour - the kill shot.
5. **The ending:** "The math has spoken. And it's ..." (`endings`, `closers`).

`_clean` lines at the clean roast levels; `_18` (starred in public; the private uncensored copy when it's there) at the 18+ levels. gemma fills only one short slot (the equation's name, acronym, its three things, a nickname) and the colour line.

### equation roast

On the menu from share **0**.

1. **openers**: Doc's opener - e.g. "OH MY GOD, YOU ABSOLUTE LEGEND OF THE CALCULATOR."
2. **leadins**: the lead-in, with his nickname for the asker - e.g. "Buckle up, {nickname} - here's the peer-reviewed equation."
3. **equations**: the equation, (A × B) ÷ (C) - e.g. "{name} ({acr}) = ({a} × {b}) ÷ ({c})"
4. **plugins**: the plug-in, meme numbers - e.g. "Plugging in: {a} = {va}. {b} = {vb}. {c} = {vc}."
5. **breaks**: the ERROR - e.g. "So {acr} = ({va} × {vb}) ÷ {vc}. {error}"
6. **hiddens**: the hidden multiplier - e.g. "But wait - there's a hidden variable you forgot: the {multiplier} ({ma}). Yours is currently sitting at {number}."
7. **corrected**: the corrected formula - e.g. "Corrected formula: Real {name} = {acr} × (1/{ma}) = {result}."
8. **colour**: gemma's one escalated-metaphor line (asked ahead)
9. **his reply** (the value point)
10. **the ending**

## Adding a recipe

1. Put its lines in pools here (one line per entry; `_clean` / `_18`).
2. Add its chain to `docbs.chains`: `{"name": "...", "min_share": 0, "beats": ["roasts", "riddles|paradoxes", "corrected"]}`.
3. `wpr 2`. Locked by `TestDocBSChainsByDial`.
